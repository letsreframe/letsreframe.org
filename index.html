We are on a mission

We celebrate the many ways a life can grow, and learn to see ourselves and one another in a new light.

We are dedicated to transforming lives by addressing limiting beliefs, taboos, stereotypes and biases, and promoting well-being for all.

See how

We want to reframe

  • Stories for kids

    By creating GROWING TOGETHER, a children's book series that promotes empathy, inclusion and a love of reading. Seven titles, available in 18 countries.

  • Preterm birth

    By celebrating every life that begins early, and placing neonatal equipment in hospitals in need. Since 2024, 107 babies have been cared for in the incubator we donated to UKC Kragujevac.
